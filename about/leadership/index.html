The People Behind Windek

Operations, commercial, people and finance leadership with a shared commitment to indigenous excellence in Nigeria's energy sector.

Emmanuel Uwandu

Chief Operating Officer

Emmanuel leads Windek's operations across the energy value chain, with over 10 years of experience in the oil and gas sector spanning strategic operations, project delivery and expansion.

Areas of focus

Operations strategy, project delivery, upstream and midstream expansion

Motunrayo Adeogun

Head of Business Development

Motunrayo drives Windek's growth strategy and manages stakeholder relationships across the energy value chain, from joint venture operators to LPG distributors and industrial consumers.

Areas of focus

Commercial growth, key accounts, partnerships and tender participation

Joy Makanjuola

Head of Human Resources

Joy leads people operations at Windek, responsible for talent development, organisational design and the local content programmes that build Nigerian technical capability.

Areas of focus

Talent, organisational development, local content and training

Dorcas Oloniyo

Head of Account

Dorcas oversees Windek's financial operations, including project costing, commercial controls and contract administration across active engagements.

Areas of focus

Financial control, project costing and contract administration
