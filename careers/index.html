Careers

Build Energy Infrastructure With Us

We develop local engineering and project capability in Nigeria. That means real technical work, site exposure and long-term project continuity for people who join us.

Why Join Windek

Capability built locally, careers built with it

As a 100% indigenous operator, our growth depends on developing Nigerian engineering, project management and technical workforce capability. That means our people get work on substantial infrastructure projects rather than short task-based assignments.

Our flagship LPG Terminal Facility is a multi-year build with genuine scope for mechanical, civil, instrumentation, HSE and project controls experience. Upstream operations work offers field exposure across drilling, completion and production support.

Operations and Engineering

Well operations, project engineering, EPCI delivery, civil and mechanical works, and HSE roles on active sites.

Commercial and Business Development

Tender management, key account management, supplier engagement and commercial proposals.

Technical training and internships

Structured industrial training placements for students of engineering, geoscience and technical disciplines.

Current vacancies

We do not currently have a list of open positions published on this page. Rather than advertise generic listings, we keep a live talent pipeline and contact people when a role matching their profile becomes available.
